A Cross-Sector Synthesis of the AI & Semiconductor Hardware Stack

Ten sectors, two hundred and thirty papers, one continuously verified reading of where the bottlenecks actually are

Abstract

  • Retrospective covering ten sectors: GPUs, CPUs, memory, chip fabrication, AI accelerators, advanced packaging, photonics, interconnects, datacenter hardware, and edge AI hardware.
  • Approximately two hundred and forty-two primary sources published between November 2025 and May 2026, validated against six recency, methodology, and attribution criteria.
  • A cross-sector permutation pass produces ranked non-consensus findings, each independently checked against current sell-side, vendor, and trade-press consensus by a separate verification agent.
  • The pipeline re-executes every five hours; already-priced-in findings are cleanly removed from the synthesis to preserve signal.
  • Run #4 produced zero VERIFIED-NOT-PRICED-IN findings; fifteen claims across six cross-sector findings and nine market opportunities survive as partially priced — including a ≥3-year structural High-NA EUV foundry bifurcation (TSMC’s entire 2029 roadmap skips High-NA, confirmed by the April 2026 TSMC North America Technology Symposium) and Apple’s early-stage foundry discussions with Intel and Samsung as the first credible demand-side relief mechanism for TSMC N2 AI chip supply.
  • No items were retired this cycle (zero ALREADY-PRICED-IN findings in Run #4). Running total: thirteen retired across all runs.
