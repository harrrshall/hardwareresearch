# Paper 009: VLSI 2025 — Outer Wall Forksheet: Bridging Nanosheet and CFET Architectures

**Source ID:** 29, 39  
**Authors:** imec Research Team  
**Venue:** 45th Symposium on VLSI Technology & Circuits 2025, Kyoto, Japan  
**Date:** June 2025  
**Tags:** GAAFET, nanosheet  
**URL:** https://www.eetimes.com/vlsi-2025-outer-wall-forksheet-bridges-nanosheet-and-cfet-architectures/

## Abstract / Summary

At VLSI 2025 (Kyoto, June 8-12), imec presented simulations and architecture analysis of the "outer wall forksheet" transistor design, positioning it as the bridge between the current nanosheet era and the future CFET era. The outer wall forksheet addresses manufacturability concerns of the original inner wall forksheet while delivering superior gate control and enabling full channel stress.

## Key Technical Data

- **Target node:** A10 (~1.0nm class) — extends nanosheet era two additional generations
- **CFET introduction:** Projected A7 (~0.7nm class), ~2033 commercial production
- **Forksheet principle:** NMOS and PMOS nanosheets share a common dielectric wall; n-p pair separated by wall thickness only
- **Outer wall advantage vs. inner wall:**
  - Easier fabrication (outer wall accessible for stress deposition)
  - Superior gate control (wrap-around geometry maintained)
  - Connected n-p gate (single gate patterning step per pair)
  - Full channel stress achievable (no inner wall blocking)
- **Cell height reduction:** Forksheet enables ~5-10% smaller standard cell height vs. standalone nanosheet
- **Density projection:** ~15% area improvement vs. nanosheet at equivalent gate pitch

## Key Findings

1. The outer wall redesign resolves the key fabrication objection to forksheet — the inner wall was blocking stress liner deposition and gate contact.
2. imec's roadmap extends nanosheet to A10, forksheet to A7-ish boundary, then CFET from A7 onwards.
3. Connected n-p gate removes one lithography step per transistor pair — significant cost reduction.
4. Forksheet is most attractive for logic-dense applications where cell height minimization is prioritized.
5. CFET mass production remains ~8-9 years away (2033); industry must extend nanosheet/forksheet for the intervening generations.

VLSI 2025 occurred in June 2025, just before the research window. The findings are being incorporated into foundry roadmap planning observable during the window, particularly for A10 and beyond strategies.
