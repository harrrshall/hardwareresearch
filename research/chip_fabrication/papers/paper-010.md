## Abstract / Summary

Atomic layer deposition (ALD) has become a critical enabling technology for sub-3nm semiconductor manufacturing. 2025 research covers three frontier areas: area-selective deposition (ASD) for self-aligned patterning, atomic layer etching (ALE) for atomic-precision material removal, and atomic layer annealing (ALA) for low-thermal-budget interface optimization. The ALD market reached $2.70 billion in 2025, growing at 12.3% CAGR driven by 2nm logic and 3D NAND demands.

## Key Technical Data

- **ALD market size (2025):** USD 2.70 billion
- **ALD market projection (2033):** USD 6.87 billion (12.3% CAGR)
- **ASD applications:** Self-aligned gate spacer, self-aligned contact formation — enabling <5nm CD control without lithography
- **ALE precision:** Atomic-scale material removal with etch rates of 0.1-0.5 Å/cycle
- **ALA benefit:** <400°C thermal budget interface formation — compatible with 3D monolithic integration
- **Key materials (2025):**
  - High-k dielectrics: HfO2, ZrO2, Al2O3 (gate oxide, DRAM capacitors)
  - Metal gates: TiN, TaN, W via ALD for GAA inner spacer filling
  - Ferroelectric: HZO (HfZrO) for MIM capacitors and FeRAM
  - 2D materials: MoS2, WS2 via ALD for potential 2D FET channels

## Key Findings

1. ASD enables "bottom-up" patterning where material is selectively deposited on one surface but not another — reducing dependence on multi-patterning EUV steps.
2. ALE with sub-Å per cycle control is essential for precisely thinning nanosheet channels to target thickness (5-8 nm) without roughness.
3. ALA enables interface passivation at thermal budgets compatible with back-end processes in 3D monolithic stacks.
4. 2D material deposition via ALD (MoS2, WS2) is advancing for potential integration as ultra-thin channel layers in post-nanosheet FETs.
5. Inner spacer ALD for GAA is one of the most challenging manufacturing steps — requires conformal filling of <5nm gaps between stacked nanosheets.

## Relevance to Research Window (2025-11-22 to 2026-05-22)

ALD/ALE advances are directly enabling N2 and 18A manufacturing during the research window. Inner spacer ALD for GAA nanosheet stacks is active production process at TSMC and Samsung.
