# Paper 004: ASML EXE:5200B — First High-NA EUV System for Volume Manufacturing

**Source ID:** 11, 12  
**Authors:** ASML Technology Team  
**Venue:** ASML Q2 2025 Financial Report / TrendForce  
**Date:** July 2025  
**Tags:** high-NA-EUV  
**URL:** https://www.trendforce.com/news/2025/07/17/news-asml-confirms-first-high-na-euv-exe5200-shipment-reportedly-prepping-for-intels-14a-in-2027/

## Abstract / Summary

ASML confirmed delivery of the first EXE:5200B system for high-NA EUV lithography in Q2 2025, marking the shift from R&D-grade EXE:5000 systems to production-ready High-NA EUV. The EXE:5200B introduces 60% productivity gains over the EXE:5000 and supports sub-2nm logic patterning. This delivery signals that High-NA EUV is now commercially ready for volume manufacturing.

## Key Technical Data

- **Numerical aperture:** 0.55 NA (vs. 0.33 NA for standard EUV NXE-series)
- **EXE:5200B throughput:** >175 wafers per hour
- **EXE:5000 throughput:** ~90-100 wph (R&D baseline)
- **Productivity improvement:** 60% over EXE:5000
- **Wavelength:** 13.5 nm EUV (same as standard; NA change, not wavelength)
- **Resolution:** ~8nm half-pitch (vs. ~13nm for 0.33 NA EUV)
- **NXE:3800E (standard EUV):** 195 wph, upgradable to 230 wph; Q1 2026 upgrade package released
- **System cost:** High-NA EUV systems cost >$350M each
- **First shipment:** EXE:5000 to Intel in 2023 (R&D); EXE:5200B for production in 2025

## Key Findings

1. EXE:5200B delivery in Q2 2025 transitions High-NA EUV from R&D/risk phase to volume manufacturing readiness.
2. Intel plans to use High-NA EUV (EXE:5000 initially) for 18A proof points in 2025, continuing into 14A production.
3. ASML confirmed EXE:5200 is being prepared for Intel 14A production node (~2027), enabling sub-1.4nm equivalent patterning.
4. Standard EUV (NXE:3800E) remains primary workhorse for 2nm-class nodes; High-NA reserved for A-series angstrom nodes.
5. High-NA EUV can print single-exposure features previously requiring double-patterning — a significant process simplification.

## Relevance to Research Window (2025-11-22 to 2026-05-22)

EXE:5200B shipped Q2 2025. Through the research window, Intel's use of High-NA EUV for 18A process validation and 14A development are active. NXE:3800E upgrade package (Q1 2026) also within window.
