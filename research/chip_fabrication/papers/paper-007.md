## Abstract / Summary

TSMC's CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate) advanced packaging platform has become the central infrastructure bottleneck for AI chip production. This analysis covers the capacity ramp from 35K wpm in late 2024 to a projected 130K wpm by end 2026, the technical differentiation between CoWoS-S and CoWoS-L variants, and NVIDIA's outsized demand for CoWoS-L in support of Blackwell-class GPU architectures.

## Key Technical Data

- **CoWoS capacity (late 2024):** ~35,000 wafers per month (wpm)
- **CoWoS capacity (late 2025):** ~75-80,000 wpm
- **CoWoS capacity target (end 2026):** 120-130,000 wpm
- **NVIDIA demand 2025:** ~400,000 wafers annually (CoWoS-L dominant, B200/B300)
- **NVIDIA demand 2026:** ~700,000 wafers annually (75%+ growth)
- **NVIDIA share of CoWoS capacity:** >70% of TSMC's CoWoS-L allocation
- **Outsourced volume 2026:** 240-270K wafers/year to Amkor (180-190K) and SPIL (60-80K)
- **CoWoS-S:** Monolithic silicon interposer, up to 3.3x reticle (~2700 mm²), yield-limited at large sizes
- **CoWoS-L:** RDL + Local Silicon Interconnect (LSI) reconstituted interposer; larger format possible
- **2029 roadmap:** 14+ reticle packages; 24 HBM5E stacks; 48x compute increase projected

## Key Findings

1. CoWoS capacity has become the single biggest constraint on AI GPU supply — leading to strategic outsourcing to OSATs.
2. Transition from CoWoS-S to CoWoS-L reflects the need for larger interposers that avoid the monolithic silicon yield penalty.
3. TSMC's SoIC (System-on-Integrated-Chips) 3D stacking is emerging alongside CoWoS for memory-logic integration.
4. CoWoS co-packaged optics (COUPE) announced in 2025 adds optical interconnect capability to the packaging stack.
5. The 2029 roadmap target of 14+ reticle-size packages would accommodate >1 THz aggregate memory bandwidth.

## Relevance to Research Window (2025-11-22 to 2026-05-22)

CoWoS capacity ramp from 75K to 130K wpm is actively progressing through the research window. NVIDIA Blackwell demand is the primary driver. Outsourcing to Amkor/SPIL commenced in this period.
