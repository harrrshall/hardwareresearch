## Abstract / Summary

TSMC presented its full N2 platform technology paper at IEDM 2025, titled "2nm Platform Technology Featuring Energy-Efficient Nanosheet Transistors and Interconnects Co-Optimized with 3DIC for AI, HPC and Mobile SoC Applications." This is TSMC's first deployment of Gate-All-Around (GAA) nanosheet transistors, marking the transition from FinFET scaling. The paper covered transistor architecture, SRAM density, reliability qualification, and 3DIC co-optimization.

## Key Technical Data

- **Transistor architecture:** Nanosheet GAAFET (Gate-All-Around Field-Effect Transistor)
- **Gate length:** 22 nm
- **Channel width:** 45 nm (nanosheet width)
- **Ion improvement:** ~70% for NFET, ~110% for PFET vs. N3E baseline
- **SRAM density:** 38 Mb/mm² (world record at time of publication)
- **Logic density:** >1.15x chip density increase vs. N3E
- **Performance:** 15% speed gain at iso-power vs. N3E
- **Power:** 30-35% power reduction at iso-performance vs. N3E
- **Yield:** ~65-70% at volume ramp; full qualification completed 2025
- **Production:** Volume production Q4 2025; mass production ramp 2026

## Key Findings

1. N2 transitions TSMC from FinFET (N3/N3E/N3P) to GAA nanosheet architecture.
2. SRAM bitcell achieves world-record density of 38 Mb/mm², critical for AI workloads.
3. CFET milestone: first functional 101-stage 3D monolithic CFET ring oscillator demonstrated (future roadmap signal).
4. BEOL interconnect features scalable copper-based RDL, flat passivation, and TSV integration for 3DIC compatibility.
5. N2 wafer-level reliability requirements met; full qualification in 2025 preceding mass production.
