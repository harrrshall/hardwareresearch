## Abstract / Summary

EUV pellicles — ultra-thin protective membranes placed over the photomask to prevent particle contamination — are a critical yield enabler for EUV lithography. TSMC's in-house developed EUV pellicle (reported mid-2024) demonstrated 4x longer lifespan and 4.5x more wafer output per pellicle vs. prior generation. Mitsui Chemicals is commercializing next-generation carbon nanotube (CNT) pellicles with 97% EUV transmission, targeting 5000 sheets/year production capacity.

## Key Technical Data

**TSMC In-House Pellicle (2024-2025):**
- Lifespan improvement: 4x vs. previous generation
- Wafer output per pellicle: 4.5x improvement
- Defect reduction: 80x reduction in pellicle-induced defects
- EUV throughput contribution: 30x improvement over 6 years of system-level optimization

**CNT Pellicle (Mitsui / ASML Next-Gen):**
- Transmission: 97% EUV transmission
- Temperature durability: >1,500°C (vs. ~400°C for SiN-based pellicles)
- Target production: 5,000 sheets/year
- Status (2025): Commercialization underway
- Previous generation (polysilicon): ~83% → 90% transmission

**System-Level Context:**
- TSMC's combined EUV optimization (pellicle + scanner tuning): 30x wafer production improvement over 6 years while reducing power consumption by 24%

## Key Findings

1. Pellicle transmission efficiency directly multiplies EUV throughput — a 90% vs. 97% pellicle represents a meaningful dose budget recovery at the scanner.
2. CNT pellicles' >1500°C thermal durability enables use with high-power EUV sources needed for High-NA systems without pellicle degradation.
3. TSMC's 80x defect reduction from in-house pellicle design demonstrates that system integration (foundry + tool) delivers more than the sum of parts.
4. Pellicle availability is a supply chain risk — Mitsui's 5000 sheets/year capacity needs to scale with EUV fleet size (currently ~1000+ NXE systems deployed globally).
5. For High-NA EUV, pellicle development is even more critical — 0.55 NA systems require pellicles that can survive higher power density exposure.

## Relevance to Research Window (2025-11-22 to 2026-05-22)

CNT pellicle commercialization is occurring during the research window. TSMC's optimized pellicle is in active production use at N2 fabs. ASML released NXE:3800E upgrade package (230 wph) in Q1 2026, partially enabled by improved pellicle performance.
