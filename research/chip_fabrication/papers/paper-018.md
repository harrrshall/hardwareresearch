## Abstract / Summary

IEDM 2025 featured a series of breakthrough fabrication papers covering three forward-looking areas: (1) TSMC's first functional CFET integrated circuits (ring oscillators and SRAM), (2) Intel's MIM capacitor materials (HfZrO) for decoupling in advanced nodes, and (3) imec/KU Leuven/Intel 2D FET back-end-of-line integration. Together, these papers chart the trajectory beyond GAA nanosheet toward CFET and 2D material FETs in the angstrom era.

## Key Technical Data

**TSMC CFET milestones:**
- First functional 101-stage 3D monolithic CFET ring oscillator
- World's smallest 6T SRAM bit cell
- Gate pitch: <48 nm between complementary NFET/PFET
- Technologies: Nanosheet Cut Isolation (NCI), Butt Contact (BCT) interconnection
- Status: Functional circuits (not production-ready; ~8-10 years to production)

**Intel MIM capacitors (IEDM 2025):**
- Material: HfZrO (ferroelectric hafnium zirconium oxide)
- Application: On-die decoupling capacitors in 18A process
- Benefit: Higher capacitance density vs. TiN/TaN conventional MIM stacks

**2D FET BEOL Integration (imec/Intel):**
- Materials: MoS2, WS2 (transition metal dichalcogenides)
- Integration: Back-end-of-line processes (compatible with existing CMOS)
- Status: Research-level; targeting future sub-1nm FET channels

## Key Findings

1. TSMC's working CFET ring oscillator is the first functional proof of concept — demonstrates the architecture is viable, not just theoretical.
2. CFET's <48nm gate pitch is below the fundamental nanosheet pitch limit, confirming density advantage.
3. Intel's HZO MIM capacitors enable ferroelectric operation at 1nm equivalent oxide thickness — potential path to embedded NVM alongside logic.
4. 2D material FET integration at BEOL opens a path to "3D logic" where transistors are built on top of copper interconnect layers.
5. All three IEDM 2025 topics represent the 2030-2035 generation — current 2nm volume production is still 10+ years ahead of these concepts.
