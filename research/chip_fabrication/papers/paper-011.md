# Paper 011: TSMC N3P — Third-Generation 3nm Node Performance and Density

**Source ID:** 17, 18  
**Authors:** TSMC Technology Team  
**Venue:** TSMC Technology Disclosures / Tom's Hardware  
**Date:** 2025  
**Tags:** 2nm, nanosheet, yield  
**URL:** https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/tsmcs-3nm-update-n3p-in-production-n3x-on-track

## Abstract / Summary

TSMC's N3P represents the third-generation 3nm FinFET node (following N3 and N3E), entering volume production in H2 2024 and serving as the dominant 3nm node throughout 2025 prior to N2's ramp. N3P achieves 224 MTr/mm² transistor density through an optical shrink and process optimization versus N3E (216 MTr/mm²), while maintaining FinFET architecture. N3P is a transitional bridge node before full GAA adoption at N2.

## Key Technical Data

- **Transistor architecture:** FinFET (same as N3, N3E — not GAA)
- **Transistor density (N3P):** ~224 MTr/mm² (High Density library)
- **Transistor density (N3E):** ~216 MTr/mm²
- **N3P density improvement vs. N3E:** +4%
- **N3P performance vs. N3E:** +5% speed at iso-power
- **N3P power vs. N3E:** -5 to -10% at iso-performance
- **N3E performance vs. N5:** +18% speed or >30% power reduction
- **N3X vs. N3P:** +5% performance or -7% power (high-voltage variant for HPC)
- **N3P production status:** Volume production H2 2024; HVM in 2025
- **Gate length:** ~17-18nm effective channel length (FinFET)

## Key Findings

1. N3P represents the practical ceiling of FinFET scaling at TSMC — N2 onwards moves to GAA nanosheet.
2. The 224 MTr/mm² density of N3P required optical shrink (tight pitch lithography) rather than architectural change.
3. N3E became the baseline for Apple A18 Pro (iPhone 16 Pro, 2024); N3P is positioned for Apple A19 (iPhone 17, 2025).
4. N3X (high-voltage variant) targets HPC/AI accelerator applications with relaxed density but maximum drive current.
5. The FinFET-to-GAA transition at N2 introduces entirely new process steps (nanosheet release, inner spacer, wrap-around gate) — higher complexity but better short-channel control.

## Relevance to Research Window (2025-11-22 to 2026-05-22)

N3P remains in high-volume production during the research window for non-leading-edge products. The node serves as the performance/cost baseline against which N2 improvements are measured during the window.
