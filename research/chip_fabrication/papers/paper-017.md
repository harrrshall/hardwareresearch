## Abstract / Summary

TSMC's 2nm production is distributed across two primary facilities: Fab 20 (Baoshan, Hsinchu) and Fab 22 (Kaohsiung). This paper synthesizes the production ramp timeline, capacity targets, and customer allocation for TSMC's N2 node. Apple has secured approximately 50% of initial N2 capacity for the A20 Bionic chip (iPhone 18), with NVIDIA and other AI customers absorbing the remainder.

## Key Technical Data

- **Fab 20 (Baoshan) capacity trajectory:**
  - Pilot: 3,000-3,500 wpm (early 2025)
  - Q4 2025: 20,000-25,000 wpm
  - End 2026: 60,000-65,000 wpm
- **Fab 22 (Kaohsiung) capacity trajectory:**
  - Q4 2025: 25,000-30,000 wpm
  - End 2026: 60,000-65,000 wpm
- **Combined N2 capacity:**
  - End 2025: ~50,000 wpm
  - End 2026: 120,000-130,000 wpm
- **Wafer cost:** ~$30,000 per N2 wafer
- **Apple allocation:** ~50% of initial 2025-2026 N2 capacity for A20 Bionic
- **2026 booking status:** Both Baoshan and Kaohsiung N2 capacity completely sold out
- **Production start (volume):** Q4 2025 (N2); N2P volume production H2 2026

## Key Findings

1. TSMC's simultaneous dual-fab ramp (Baoshan + Kaohsiung) is unprecedented for a new process node — enabled by Apple's guaranteed demand.
2. The $30,000/wafer N2 cost is ~2x N3E ($14,000-16,000), reflecting the GAA process complexity premium.
3. Kaohsiung Fab 22 was originally planned as an advanced node HPC/AI hub; Apple's A20 allocation anchors the initial production economics.
4. 2026 N2 capacity is fully subscribed as of mid-2025, creating a waiting list dynamic that benefits TSMC's pricing power.
5. N2P (with BSPDN) is planned for H2 2026 production at the same facilities, building on established N2 process infrastructure.

## Relevance to Research Window (2025-11-22 to 2026-05-22)

N2 volume production is actively ramping throughout the research window. The critical transition from 50K wpm to 130K wpm occurs within and immediately after the window. Apple A20 Bionic chips are being produced during this period.
