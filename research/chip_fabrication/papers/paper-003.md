## Abstract / Summary

Samsung's SF2 (Samsung Foundry 2nm) process is the company's second-generation GAA node after SF3 (3nm), leveraging its Multi-Bridge Channel FET (MBCFET) nanosheet architecture. Samsung reported a yield of 55-60% for SF2 in November 2025, improving to 70% for its performance-optimized SF2P variant by January 2026. The Exynos 2600 is the industry's first 2nm GAA application processor.

## Key Technical Data

- **Transistor architecture:** MBCFET (Multi-Bridge Channel FET) — nanosheet GAA variant
- **SF2 performance vs. SF3 (3nm GAA):** +5% performance, +8% power efficiency, -5% area
- **SF2P vs. SF2:** +12% clock speed, +25% power efficiency, -8% die area
- **SF2 yield (Nov 2025):** 55-60%
- **SF2P yield (Jan 2026):** 70% — stable mass production threshold reached
- **Key product:** Exynos 2600 — world's first 2nm GAA AP, announced Dec 2025; Galaxy Unpacked 2026
- **AI performance uplift:** 113% AI performance increase for Exynos 2600 vs predecessor
- **Customer wins:** Tesla AI6 contract ($16.5B); DeepX 2nm (Korea AI startup); Charbright 4nm

## Key Findings

1. Samsung's early adoption of GAA at 3nm gave it a data advantage — SF2P's yield improvement leveraged 4 years of GAA telemetry from SF3.
2. SF2P yield jump from ~55% to 70% within 2 months signals rapid process maturation in late 2025/early 2026.
3. Tesla's $16.5B AI6 chip contract is the largest known advanced-node foundry win in this research window.
4. Samsung plans to double 2nm capacity by end of 2026 including Taylor, Texas fab contributions.
5. SF2Z (with BSPDN) is planned for 2027 production, extending the 2nm node family with backside power.
