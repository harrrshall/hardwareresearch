# Paper 002: Immersion Cooling Market — Single-Phase vs Two-Phase Technology, 2025–2026

**Tags:** immersion-cooling, single-phase, two-phase, thermal-management
**Source:** ResearchNester, IDTechEx, ScienceDirect
**Date:** 2025–2026
**Relevance:** High

## Summary

Immersion cooling is growing rapidly but remains a smaller fraction of cooling deployments than direct-to-chip. The market was valued at USD 3.98 billion in 2025, projected to reach USD 19.63 billion by 2035 at a 17.3% CAGR. Single-phase and two-phase approaches serve distinct use cases with substantially different heat flux capabilities.

## Single-Phase Immersion Cooling

- Servers are fully submerged in non-conductive dielectric fluid (typically mineral oil, synthetic hydrocarbon, or engineered fluorocarbon)
- Fluid remains liquid throughout; heat is removed via heat exchanger at the tank surface
- Sustained heat flux: approximately 250 W/cm² at chip surface
- Mineral oil: lowest cost (~46% market share 2024), good dielectric, lower thermal conductivity
- Shell introduced DLC Fluid S3 (Dec 2024): synthetic hydrocarbon with 25% higher thermal conductivity than mineral oil
- ScienceDirect study (2025) compared FC-40 dielectric vs mineral oil in single-phase systems
- Single-phase expected to hold 63.7% share by 2035, driven by operational simplicity

## Two-Phase Immersion Cooling

- Coolant vaporises at chip surface (pool boiling); vapour condenses on a heat exchanger, returns as liquid
- Heat flux capability: ~1,500 W/cm², potentially higher
- Two-phase segment: 66.21% of immersion market in 2026 by value (superior efficiency premium)
- Two-phase global market: USD 431.4M in 2025, USD 496.7M in 2026
- IDTechEx projects two-phase cold plate cooling (hybrid variant) takes off 2026–2027

## Key Infrastructure Differences

| Metric | Single-Phase | Two-Phase |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| Heat flux | ~250 W/cm² | ~1,500 W/cm² |
| CAPEX | Lower | Higher |
| Fluid cost | Low (mineral oil) | High (engineered fluids) |
| Maintenance | Simpler | More complex |
| PUE achievable | ~1.05 | ~1.03 |

## 2025–2026 Developments

- Modine acquired TMG Core immersion IP assets in Jan 2024; deploying at scale 2025
- 3M Novec fluid phase-out has driven operators back toward hydrocarbon fluids
- New tank designs accommodate multiple 132 kW GB200 NVL72-equivalent compute nodes per tank
- Cryptocurrency mining operations are proving single-phase at hyperscale, providing data for AI deployment

## Implications

Two-phase offers near-theoretical efficiency but at higher cost and complexity. Single-phase is operationally preferred for retrofits and co-location deployments. Neither approach has displaced direct-to-chip as the primary AI cooling paradigm by 2026 due to rack form-factor constraints.
