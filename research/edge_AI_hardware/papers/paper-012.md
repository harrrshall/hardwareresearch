# paper-012: Samsung Exynos 2600 — World's First 2nm Mobile Chip

**Tags:** `mobile-NPU` `manufacturing`
**Type:** Product/Technical Announcement
**Source:** Samsung Semiconductor + GSMArena
**URL:** https://semiconductor.samsung.com/processor/mobile-processor/exynos-2600/
**Date:** 2025-12-19

---

## Summary

Samsung announces the **Exynos 2600**, the world's first mobile application processor fabricated using a 2nm Gate-All-Around (GAA) process (Samsung Foundry SF2). Marks a pivotal milestone in mobile AI processing with 113% generative AI improvement over the Exynos 2500.

## Manufacturing Process

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Process node | Samsung SF2 (2nm GAA) |
| Transistor density | ~231 MTr/mm² |
| vs TSMC N3P | 3-22% density advantage |
| Samsung SF2 yield | ~40% (vs TSMC N2: ~60%) |
| Announcement date | December 19, 2025 |

## AI/NPU Specifications

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Generative AI improvement vs Exynos 2500 | **113%** |
| Architecture improvement | Reduced power AND latency for gen AI |
| AI model optimization partnership | Nota AI (NetsPresso platform) |
| Model size reduction capability | 90% with maintained accuracy |

## AI Architecture Innovations

### NPU Advances
- Next-generation NPU architecture over Exynos 2500's 59 TOPS
- Improved vector engine for gen AI acceleration
- Support for on-device diffusion models, LLMs, and multimodal AI

### Nota AI Partnership
- NetsPresso platform integrated for model optimization
- Reduces AI model parameters by up to 90%
- Maintains accuracy while enabling models previously too large for mobile deployment

## Context: Exynos 2500 vs 2600

| Chip | Process | NPU TOPS | Gen AI vs Prior |
|------|---------|----------|-----------------|
| Exynos 2500 | 3nm GAA (SF3) | 59 TOPS | +39% |
| Exynos 2600 | 2nm GAA (SF2) | TBD (>59) | +113% |

## Deployment

- Expected debut: Samsung Galaxy S26 series (Q1 2026)
- Samsung beat Apple to 2nm mobile chip by ~9 months
- TSMC N2 Apple A19X/M5 expected late 2026

## Strategic Implications

Samsung's 2nm GAA leadership (despite lower yields vs TSMC) demonstrates that yield is not the only competitive dimension. The 113% gen AI improvement positions Exynos 2600 as a serious contender vs Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 in the AI performance dimension.
