2. **262 TOPS photonic HDC**: arXiv preprint — methodology plausible but not yet peer-reviewed at journal level.

4. **NTT 455 Tbps over 1000 km**: System-level demonstration, not component-level metric; transmission uses multi-carrier WDM techniques.
5. **TSMC COUPE 5-10× power improvement**: Vendor claim, benchmarked against undefined baseline; actual savings depend heavily on pluggable reference choice.
