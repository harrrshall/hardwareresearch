# Paper 014: OFC 2026 — AI Forces Optics Into the Spotlight (Conference Report)

**Tags:** optical-interconnect, co-packaged-optics, silicon-photonics  
**Date:** March/April 2026  
**Source:** Woodside Capital / WWT / TSPASemiconductor

## Summary

The Optical Fiber Communication Conference 2026 (Los Angeles) drew ~18,000 attendees with AI data center infrastructure as the single dominant theme. Key technology threads: CPO at scale, 1.6T/3.2T advances, OCS for GPU fabrics, LPO mainstream, and MicroLED emergence.

## Conference Scale

- **Attendees:** ~18,000 (up from ~17,000 at OFC 2025)
- **Location:** Los Angeles Convention Center
- **Core theme:** AI data center optical backbone

## Key Technical Themes

### 1. Co-Packaged Optics (CPO)
- Broadcom TH6 Davisson (102.4 Tbps) shipping since October 2025
- TSMC COUPE volume production status confirmed
- NVIDIA Spectrum-X Photonics Ethernet availability in 2026
- CPO power savings of 60-73% vs pluggable confirmed at production scale

### 2. 1.6T and 3.2T Transceivers
- 200G/lane EML enabling 1.6T mainstream
- Source Photonics, Coherent, Lumentum showing 1.6T products
- 400G/lane DSPs demonstrated (enabling 3.2T)
- Cignal AI: 5M+ 1.6T units projected in 2026; module pricing $1,300-1,500

### 3. Optical Circuit Switching (OCS)
- MEMS-based OCS for GPU cluster topology
- Lumentum, Polatis, Huawei showing OCS products
- Sub-microsecond circuit provisioning for disaggregated racks
- OCS overlaying packet network for large training job bursts

### 4. Linear Pluggable Optics (LPO)
- LPO MSA 100G/lane spec finalized
- 800G LPO (<8.5W vs >16W for DSP modules)
- Arista, Cisco, Juniper deploying LPO in AI cluster ToR switches

### 5. MicroLED Interconnects
- Avicena LightBundle eKit launched (March 2026)
- 80 fJ/bit demonstrated at 4G/lane
- Positioned for die-to-die and rack-scale AI scale-up

### 6. Hollow-Core Fiber
- Microsoft/Lumenisity: 0.091 dB/km demonstrated
- Ciena CTO: HCF "future generation" for low-latency interconnect
- Early deployments in high-frequency trading and AI scale-across

## Industry Consensus

All AI data center interconnects will be optical within 5 years. Hyperscale adoption roadmap:
- 2026: CPO initial deployments, 1.6T scaling
- 2027: Mainstream AI fabric adoption
- 2028: Broad CPO/OCS adoption
- 2030: Full photonic fabric in hyperscale AI factories
