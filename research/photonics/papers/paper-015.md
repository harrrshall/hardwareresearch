# Paper 015: imec iSiPP300/iSiPP400 — 300mm Silicon Photonics Platform for 400G/lane

**Tags:** silicon-photonics  
**Date:** 2025–2026  
**Source:** imec / TrendForce / BusinessWire  
**URL:** https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251207617476/en/UMC-Licenses-imecs-iSiPP300-Technology-to-Extend-Silicon-Photonics-Capabilities-for-Next-Generation-Connectivity

## Summary

imec developed iSiPP300 (300mm silicon photonics process) for next-generation optical transceivers and licensed it to UMC for commercial production. In parallel, iSiPP400 (400G/lane platform) is in development for mid-2026 readiness. At ECOC 2025, imec demonstrated a GeSi electro-absorption modulator (EAM) with >110 GHz bandwidth achieving 400 Gbps/lane.

## Platform Specifications

### iSiPP300 (current)
- **Wafer size:** 300 mm
- **Target rate:** 100-200G per lane
- **Licensed to:** UMC (December 2025)
- **Production:** Risk production 2026-2027 at UMC

### iSiPP400 (development)
- **Target rate:** 400G per lane
- **Development milestone:** mid-2026
- **Key device:** GeSi EAM with >110 GHz bandwidth

## ECOC 2025 Result

- **GeSi EAM:** >110 GHz C-band bandwidth on 300mm platform
- **Net data rate:** 400 Gbps per lane
- **Band:** C-band (1550nm region)
- **Integration:** CMOS-compatible 300mm process

## VLSI 2025 Result

- imec + IDLab: 7-bit, 150 GSa/s DAC in 5nm FinFET CMOS
- Enables 300 Gbps using PAM-4 modulation
- Key enabling component for next-gen coherent transceivers

## STARLight European Consortium (September 2025)

- Partners: imec + multiple EU institutions
- Goal: Silicon Photonics on 300mm for AI data centers, telecom, automotive LiDAR
- Funded under Horizon Europe

## UMC Licensing Impact

UMC's licensing of iSiPP300 adds manufacturing capacity outside TSMC ecosystem:
- Geographic diversity (Taiwan manufacturing for non-TSMC customers)
- Lower cost access to silicon photonics than leading-edge TSMC processes
- Enables ODMs and mid-tier optical module companies to access 300mm SiPho

## Significance

The iSiPP400 platform represents the first foundry process explicitly targeting 400G/lane, which is the enabling technology for 3.2T pluggable modules and next-generation CPO engines expected in 2027-2028.
