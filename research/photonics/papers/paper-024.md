## One-Sentence Claim

GlobalFoundries management expects silicon photonics revenue to roughly double in 2026 and exceed $1 billion by end-2028, positioning SiPho as a core growth engine alongside AI accelerators.

## Methodology Summary

TrendForce synthesis of GF's Q1 2026 earnings guidance and management commentary (citing GF Form 6-K FY2026 SEC filing). The revenue projection is GF's own guidance, not independently verified by TrendForce.

## Quantitative Results

- **2026 SiPho revenue trajectory:** roughly 2× 2025 levels (absolute baseline not disclosed)
- **2028 SiPho revenue target:** >$1 billion
- **Driver:** CPO adoption in AI data centers (SCALE platform launched May 4, 2026)

## Stated Limitations

- Revenue figure is management guidance, not third-party-verified
- Baseline 2025 SiPho revenue not disclosed (2x of an unknown baseline)
- Achievement depends on CPO adoption ramp, which in turn depends on EML laser supply

## Inferred Limitations

- $1B by 2028 is a material bet on the CPO deployment timeline; any delay in hyperscaler CPO adoption (e.g., due to EML laser shortfall per Finding 4 in cross_sector_alpha.md) would push this right
- Competing against TSMC COUPE volume; GF's market share trajectory is uncertain

## Architectural Significance

Provides the financial-market confirmation that the SiPho foundry race is now a revenue-material business, not just a capability demonstration. The fact that GF is projecting $1B by 2028 from SiPho alone implies a TAM that supports multiple foundries profitably — which accelerates the multi-source supply build-out identified in photonics sector analysis as the path to resolving the EML bottleneck.
