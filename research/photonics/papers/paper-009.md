# Paper 009: GlobalFoundries Acquires AMF — Largest Pure-Play Silicon Photonics Foundry

**Tags:** silicon-photonics  
**Date:** November 17, 2025  
**Source:** GlobalFoundries  
**URL:** https://gf.com/gf-press-release/globalfoundries-acquires-advanced-micro-foundry-accelerating-silicon-photonics-global-leadership-and-expanding-ai-infrastructure-portfolio/

## Summary

GlobalFoundries acquired Advanced Micro Foundry (AMF) of Singapore in November 2025, and simultaneously acquired Infinilink (Egypt) for monolithic SiPho design expertise. This made GF the largest pure-play silicon photonics foundry by revenue.

## Key Facts

- **Acquisition date:** November 2025
- **Target:** Advanced Micro Foundry (AMF), Singapore
- **AMF expertise:** 15+ years silicon photonics process IP
- **Manufacturing:** AMF's 200mm wafer fab (roadmap to 300mm)
- **Design acquisition:** Infinilink (Egypt) — monolithic SiPho design capabilities
- **GF SiPho revenue 2025:** >$200M (doubled vs 2024)
- **GF SiPho revenue 2026 target:** nearly doubled again (~$400M est.)
- **Long-term run-rate target:** $1B by end of 2028

## Technology Capabilities

- **Current production:** 200G per lane (PIC devices)
- **Roadmap:** 400G per lane and beyond
- **New platform:** iSiPP300 licensed to UMC (December 2025)
- **Center of Excellence:** Partners with A*STAR (Singapore) for next-gen materials research

## January 2025 Context

Prior to the AMF acquisition, GF announced plans for a new Advanced Packaging and Photonics Center in New York (supported by U.S. DoC investment), targeting secure onshore SiPho manufacturing for AI, defense, and aerospace.

## Strategic Implications

1. GF consolidates global SiPho capacity at a critical demand inflection
2. AMF's Singapore location adds geographic diversification
3. 200mm to 300mm transition planned — yield learning carries over
4. GF positioned as foundry for customers not using TSMC COUPE (midsize AI chip companies, defense, telecom)
