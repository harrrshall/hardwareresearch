# Paper 013: Marvell Acquires Celestial AI — $3.25B Photonic Fabric Deal

**Tags:** optical-interconnect, silicon-photonics  
**Date:** February 2, 2026  
**Source:** Marvell / SDxCentral / Optica OPN  
**URL:** https://www.marvell.com/company/newsroom/marvell-completes-acquisition-of-celestial-ai.html

## Summary

Marvell Technology completed acquisition of Celestial AI on February 2, 2026 for $3.25B ($1B cash + 27.2M MRVL shares). Celestial AI's Photonic Fabric™ technology enables 25× improvement in off-package bandwidth vs copper while simultaneously reducing power consumption, with thermally-stable operation critical for multi-kW XPU environments.

## Deal Terms

- **Total value:** $3.25B
- **Cash component:** $1B
- **Stock component:** 27.2M Marvell common shares (~$2.25B at deal close)
- **Close date:** February 2, 2026
- **Rationale:** Accelerate optical scale-up connectivity for AI XPUs

## Celestial AI Technology: Photonic Fabric™

Key differentiators:
1. **Bandwidth density:** 25× increase in off-package bandwidth vs copper interconnects
2. **Power reduction:** Substantial vs copper (specific pJ/bit figures proprietary)
3. **Thermal stability:** Operates in extreme thermal environments of multi-kW XPUs and switches
4. **Scale:** Full-stack from photonic layer to software orchestration

## Marvell Integration

Celestial AI's Photonic Fabric integrates with Marvell's:
- **Custom XPU silicon:** Marvell designs custom AI accelerators for hyperscalers
- **Electro-optics products:** Strong revenue growth from existing silicon photonics lineup
- **NVIDIA partnership:** NVIDIA invested $2B in Marvell; Photonic Fabric strengthens this relationship
- **HBM integration:** XPU+CPO+HBM on common substrate (announced at CES/GTC 2025)

## Market Context

- Optical interconnect acquisition wave: Marvell + Celestial ($3.25B), Intel OCI, Broadcom TH6, NVIDIA Photonics switches
- Ayar Labs raises $500M simultaneously
- NVIDIA invests $4B in Lumentum + Coherent for laser supply security

## Significance

The $3.25B valuation represents the largest acquisition in the optical interconnect startup space, signaling that photonic fabric technology is viewed as critical, differentiated IP for the AI hardware supply chain rather than a commodity component.
