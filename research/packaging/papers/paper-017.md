# paper-017: TSMC AP7 Advanced Packaging Facility — Chiayi Mega-Hub for SoIC, CoPoS, WMCM

**Tags:** TSMC, AP7, capacity, CoPoS, WMCM, SoIC, manufacturing
**Date Range:** 2025-Q4 – 2026-Q2
**Source IDs:** 51, 52

---

## Summary

TSMC's AP7 facility in Chiayi, Taiwan is being built as the world's largest advanced packaging hub, hosting SoIC (3D stacking), WMCM (Wafer-level Multi-Chip Module), and CoPoS (Chip-on-Panel-on-Substrate) technologies. AP7 Plant 2 entered production in early 2026 while Plant 1 is targeting 2027 mass production. TSMC's advanced packaging CapEx is projected to grow at 24% CAGR from 2025–2027.

## Technical Details

**AP7 Facility Details:**
- Location: Chiayi Science Park, Taiwan
- Total area: 15,000 m² (expanding to full 8-phase build-out)
- Plant 2 (P2): Equipment move-in H2 2025; production 2026 (Apple WMCM dedicated)
- Plant 1 (P1): Equipment move-in 2026; mass production 2027 (delayed by archaeological site discovery)

**Technology Allocation by Phase:**
| Phase | Technology | Target Date |
|---|---|---|
| P1, P3 | SoIC expansion | 2026–2027 |
| P2 | WMCM (Apple dedicated) | 2026 |
| P4 | CoPoS | End 2028 |
| Future phases | TBD (next-gen) | 2029+ |

**Technologies Hosted:**
- **SoIC:** 3D vertical stacking (hybrid bonding) — primary technology for expanding chiplet integration capacity
- **WMCM (Wafer Multi-Chip Module):** Multi-die integration at wafer level without interposer — between FOWLP and CoWoS in complexity
- **CoPoS (Chip-on-Panel-on-Substrate):** Panel-level packaging (panels > 500 mm) on organic substrate; 40% cost improvement target vs. wafer-level CoWoS

**CapEx and Investment:**
- Advanced packaging CapEx: 24% CAGR 2025–2027
- US expansion (Arizona P6 repurposed for packaging): equipment install ~2027, pre-production 2028
- TSMC committed $165B total Arizona investment (fabs + 2 advanced packaging facilities + R&D)

## Key Findings

1. AP7 is TSMC's strategic answer to the CoWoS bottleneck — scaling not just CoWoS output but diversifying to next-generation packaging formats.
2. The Apple-dedicated WMCM phase (P2) confirms Apple as TSMC's largest packaging customer and a driver of proprietary advanced packaging formats.
3. CoPoS (panel-level) at Phase 4 (end 2028) represents TSMC's long-term bet on panel economics for cost reduction.
4. TSMC's Arizona advanced packaging plant (P6 repurpose) addresses geopolitical pressure to diversify packaging outside Taiwan.
5. The 24% CapEx CAGR for advanced packaging outpaces leading-edge logic fab spending (typically 10–15% CAGR), confirming packaging as the new growth engine.

## Implications

AP7 will reshape the global advanced packaging supply map. A world's-largest single-site packaging hub in Taiwan creates concentration risk (same as logic fabs), which the Arizona plans partially address. The CoPoS ramp in 2028 is a decade-defining event — if successful, it will break the cost barrier that currently limits 2.5D/3D packaging to only the highest-value AI and HPC applications.
