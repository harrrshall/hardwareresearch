# paper-025: Heterogeneous Integration Roadmap (HIR) 2025 — Conference and Key Technical Directions

**Tags:** HIR, heterogeneous-integration, roadmap, ECTC, advanced-packaging, IEEE
**Date Range:** 2025-Q1 – 2025-Q4
**Source IDs:** 36 (ECTC context), additional HIR references

---

## Summary

The IEEE Electronics Packaging Society's Heterogeneous Integration Roadmap (HIR) 8th Annual Conference was held February 19–21, 2025, at Samsung's campus in San Jose. HIR special sessions at ECTC 2025 covered IoT/AI at the edge, AI-assisted thermal management, and photonics integration in HPC/network systems. HIR remains the definitive technical compass for 10–15-year semiconductor packaging roadmaps.

## Technical Details

**HIR 2025 Special Sessions (at ECTC 2025):**
1. IoT and AI at the Edge — miniaturization demands, low-power packaging
2. AI-Assisted Thermal Management — ML-based thermal modeling and optimization
3. Integrating Photonics in HPC and Network Systems — CPO standardization pathways

**HIR Coverage Areas:**
- Assembly and packaging technology
- Test and interconnect technology
- Heterogeneous integration: disaggregating SoCs by chiplet function
- Quantum computing packaging requirements
- Advanced substrates: glass, organic, silicon interposers
- 3D integration: hybrid bonding, TSV, monolithic

**Key HIR Technical Targets for 2025–2030:**
| Parameter | 2025 Status | 2030 Target |
|---|---|---|
| Hybrid bonding pitch (HVM) | 6 μm | 1–2 μm |
| Micro-bump pitch | 25–35 μm | 15 μm |
| RDL line/space | 2 μm | < 1 μm |
| Package-level thermal resistance | 0.15°C/W | 0.05°C/W |
| Die-to-die bandwidth density | 1 Tbps/mm² | 10 Tbps/mm² |
| Co-packaged optical bandwidth | 1.6 Tbps | 10+ Tbps |

**SEMI UCLA Packaging Manufacturing Roadmap (2025):**
- Complementary to HIR: focuses on manufacturing process scalability and cost reduction
- Panel-level packaging manufacturing to reach maturity by 2027–2028
- Wafer-level processes: transition to 450 mm wafers deferred; focus on 300 mm optimization

## Key Findings

1. HIR 8th conference attracted broad industry participation — Intel, TSMC, Samsung, IBM, Amkor, ASE, plus academia and national labs.
2. AI-assisted thermal management is a new 2025 focus area: ML models predict 3D IC hotspots during design, replacing expensive post-fab thermal testing.
3. Photonics integration is officially part of the HIR — confirming CPO/silicon photonics as a packaging (not just component) technology.
4. HIR identifies quantum computing as an emerging packaging challenge: cryogenic packaging at 4K requires entirely new interconnect and substrate approaches.
5. The SEMI/UCLA roadmap's deferral of 450mm wafers means all advanced packaging remains on 300mm wafer infrastructure.

## Implications

HIR serves as the industry's shared technical planning document. Its 2025 targets — 1–2 μm hybrid bonding pitch by 2030, 10 Tbps/mm² die-to-die bandwidth — define the innovation roadmap for all companies in the ecosystem. Achieving these targets would put packaging interconnect density within 10x of on-chip metal density, effectively making the die-to-package boundary transparent to system designers.
