# paper-012: imec Research Breakthroughs — W2W Hybrid Bonding at 250 nm Pitch and Backside Connectivity

**Tags:** hybrid-bonding, imec, W2W, D2W, research, interconnect, sub-micron
**Date Range:** 2025-Q2 – 2026-Q1
**Source IDs:** 15, 48, 49

---

## Summary

imec is the world's leading research organization for advanced semiconductor interconnect. At VLSI 2025, imec demonstrated wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonding at 250 nm interconnect pitch (feasibility study) and through-dielectric vias (TDV) at 120 nm pitch for backside connectivity. In March 2026, imec released the first-ever D2W hybrid bonding and fine-pitch RDL process design kits (PDKs) through the NanoIC pilot line.

## Technical Details

**W2W Hybrid Bonding at 250 nm:**
- Demonstrated at VLSI 2025
- Cu pad pitch: 250 nm (interconnect pitch, not bump pitch)
- Surface preparation: CMP to < 0.2 nm Ra roughness
- Overlay alignment: < 50 nm overlay error required at 250 nm pitch
- Yield challenge: copper dishing and oxide step height uniformity at sub-300 nm scale

**D2W Hybrid Bonding at 2 μm:**
- Cu/SiCN bonding surfaces (vs. SiO₂ for W2W)
- Overlay error: < 350 nm at 2 μm pitch
- This configuration supports known-good die (KGD) selection before bonding
- Enables higher-yield 3D integration for chiplet stacking

**Backside Connectivity (VLSI 2025):**
- Through-dielectric vias (TDV): 120 nm pitch on wafer backside
- Enables vertical connections from front-side active circuits to back-side redistribution layers
- Critical for CMOS 2.0 architectures: gate-all-around transistors in upper tier connected via backside power delivery

**NanoIC PDK Release (March 2026):**
- First-ever fine-pitch RDL PDK: enables circuit designers to use imec's sub-2 μm RDL process
- First-ever D2W hybrid bonding PDK: enables system designers to plan chiplet-level integration
- Accessible to imec industrial partners and research consortia

## Key Findings

1. 250 nm W2W pitch at VLSI 2025 is 24x finer than TSMC's current 6 μm HVM — represents the theoretical frontier of what's achievable by 2030.
2. TDV at 120 nm pitch enables bidirectional vertical connectivity, making true 3D-stacked compute (multi-tier logic) feasible.
3. PDK release is a major democratization step — previously, only TSMC/Samsung insiders could plan hybrid-bonded designs.
4. imec's roadmap shows 200 nm W2W pitch feasibility by 2027, potentially enabling > 25M interconnects/mm².
5. The combination of sub-300 nm hybrid bonding with backside power delivery is a cornerstone of imec's CMOS 2.0 / 3D compute platform.

## Implications

imec's results set the 5-year technology frontier for the industry. The 250 nm W2W pitch suggests that by 2030, 3D ICs could have interconnect density approaching that of on-chip metal layers — effectively making die stacking indistinguishable from multi-layer chip design. This would obsolete the die/package boundary as a conceptual and performance boundary.
