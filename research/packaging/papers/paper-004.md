# paper-004: Glass Substrate Packaging — Intel, AMD, and the Trillion-Transistor Package Vision

**Tags:** glass-substrate, Intel, AMD, advanced-packaging, interposer
**Date Range:** 2025-Q4 – 2026-Q2
**Source IDs:** 23, 24, 25, 26

---

## Summary

Glass substrates are emerging as the next-generation interposer and package substrate material, offering superior flatness, thermal stability, and electrical performance compared to organic laminates. Intel is leading commercialization (targeting mass production 2026), with AMD conducting qualification tests and Samsung/LG Innotek competing for supply.

## Technical Details

**Why Glass Over Organic Laminate:**
| Property | Organic (ABF) | Glass |
|---|---|---|
| CTE (ppm/°C) | 12–17 | 3–4 (near silicon) |
| Surface roughness | 0.5–1 μm | <0.1 μm |
| Dimensional stability | Moderate | High |
| Processing speed improvement | Baseline | +40% |
| Power consumption reduction | Baseline | -30% |
| Line/space capability | 2/2 μm | <1/1 μm target |

**Intel's Implementation:**
- Debuted first sample combining EMIB packaging with glass core substrate at NEPCON Japan, January 2026
- Package dimensions: 78×77 mm
- Silicon area supported: ~1,716 mm²
- Glass core substrate supplier: Absolics (US subsidiary of SKC)

**Cost and Yield Status (2026):**
- Current glass substrate cost premium: 2–3x over organic
- Current yield: 75–85%
- 2030 target: 40–60% cost reduction vs. current glass; yield >95%

## Key Findings

1. Intel states glass could enable 1 trillion transistors per package within this decade.
2. AMD has glass substrate samples from multiple suppliers under performance evaluation as of 2025.
3. Glass CTE near-match to silicon (~3 vs. 2.6 ppm/°C) dramatically reduces thermo-mechanical stress during assembly and thermal cycling.
4. The brittleness of glass remains the key manufacturing hurdle — handling equipment must be redesigned.
5. Transition from organic ABF substrates to glass is not yet a volume commercial reality; 2026 is a qualification milestone, not mass production for most players.

## Implications

If yield and cost challenges are resolved by 2027–2028, glass substrates could displace organic laminates for all AI accelerator and HPC packaging. The sub-1 μm line/space capability of glass enables denser RDL layers, reducing the need for silicon interposers in some 2.5D configurations — potentially lowering total package cost while improving signal integrity.
