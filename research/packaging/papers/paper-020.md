# paper-020: Advanced Packaging Market Economics — $45–52B in 2025, Growing to $80B+ by 2030

**Tags:** market, advanced-packaging, revenue, forecast, OSAT, yield
**Date Range:** 2025-Q1 – 2026-Q2
**Source IDs:** 40

---

## Summary

The global advanced semiconductor packaging market was valued at $45–52B in 2025 (varying by analyst methodology), with consensus growth to $57B in 2026 and $80B+ by 2030 at 8–10% CAGR. AI accelerators are the primary growth driver. Flip-chip packaging holds 34.7% share; 2.5D/3D is the fastest-growing segment.

**Market Segments by Technology (2025):**
- Flip-chip packaging: 34.7% share (dominant)
- 2.5D/3D packaging: fastest-growing (~15–20% CAGR)
- FOWLP/FO-PLP: $4.33B, growing at 11.2% CAGR
- Panel-level packaging: $0.35B, growing at 25.6% CAGR
- Embedded die: niche but growing at 20–22% CAGR

**OSAT Industry (2025):**
- Total OSAT market: $73.3B in 2025 (5.2% CAGR)
- Advanced packaging: >40% of total OSAT investment
- ASE Group: rolled out 3D-stacked SiP assembly line; $200M in PLP panels July 2025
- Amkor: Arizona advanced packaging campus under construction; outsourcing Intel EMIB (Songdo K5)

**Yield and Reliability Economics:**
- Advanced packaging defect rates at system level: 0.5–2% for mature CoWoS; up to 5–8% for new 3D formats
- Known-good die (KGD) testing adds $50–200 per die but reduces assembled package yield loss from 10–15% to 2–3%
- Glass substrate: current yield 75–85%; target >95% by 2030

## Key Findings

1. Advanced packaging now represents ~17% of the $300B total semiconductor packaging and assembly market.
2. CoWoS capacity constraint has driven CoWoS wafer ASP toward 7nm-level pricing — packaging pricing power is unprecedented.
3. OSAT companies are investing heavily in advanced packaging: combined ASE + Amkor advanced packaging investment exceeds $5B over 2024–2026.
4. FO-PLP at 25.6% CAGR is the fastest-growing sub-segment; driven by AI chip overflow from CoWoS.
5. Global revenues for advanced packaging forecast to grow from $46.1B (2024) to $79.4B by 2030 per Mordor Intelligence.

## Implications

The advanced packaging market is entering a "super-cycle" analogous to the wafer fab expansion of 2020–2022, but concentrated on backend processing. Unlike logic wafers, packaging capacity scaling is faster (12–18 months vs. 2–3 years for a new fab), suggesting the CoWoS shortage will partially ease by 2026–2027 — but demand may continue to outpace supply as AI clusters scale to million-GPU configurations.
