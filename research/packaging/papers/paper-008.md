# paper-008: Samsung X-Cube and H-Cube — 3D and 2.5D Packaging Ecosystem

**Tags:** X-Cube, H-Cube, Samsung, 3D-stacking, interposer, HBM
**Date Range:** 2025-Q1 – 2025-Q4
**Source IDs:** 34, 35

---

## Summary

Samsung Foundry's advanced packaging portfolio spans X-Cube (3D logic-on-logic vertical stacking via TSVs) and H-Cube (high-density 2.5D heterogeneous integration). Samsung is targeting 25 μm micro-bump pitch in 2025 and 4 μm hybrid bonding copper pitch for X-Cube in 2026, while I-CubeE (HBM-integration variant) scales to 12-HBM stacks in mass production.

## Technical Details

**X-Cube (3D Logic Stacking):**
- Uses TSVs to stack multiple logic dies vertically
- SF4/SF5 process nodes: 25 μm micro-bump pitch (2025)
- 4 μm hybrid copper bonding planned (2026)
- Reduces yield risk by disaggregating large monolithic dies
- Face-to-back stacking: logic on logic with shared power/clock

**H-Cube (2.5D HBM Integration):**
- Silicon interposer-based (similar to CoWoS-S)
- Currently supports up to 12 HBM stacks (I-CubeE) in mass production 2025
- Fine-pitch RDL under dies for signal redistribution
- Targets HPC AI accelerators as primary application

**Samsung's HBM Packaging:**
- Samsung holds ~25% of global HBM capacity share (2025)
- MR-MUF (Mass Reflow Molded Underfill) process for HBM stacking
- Plans: extend I-CubeE to 12 HBMs for mass production in 2025
- 4 μm hybrid bonding for future HBM4 stacking (2026)

## Key Findings

1. Samsung SF4 and SF5 nodes with 25 μm pitch are available in 2025 for X-Cube integration.
2. The transition to 4 μm hybrid copper bonding in 2026 would place Samsung in competitive parity with TSMC SoIC-X (6 μm HVM).
3. Samsung H-Cube with 12-HBM capability positions it directly against TSMC CoWoS for high-end AI accelerators.
4. Samsung's integrated approach (foundry + DRAM + packaging) gives it unique vertical integration advantage.
5. Samsung Foundry is also competing for Intel EMIB overflow customers, offering H-Cube as an alternative.

## Implications

Samsung's dual-pathway approach (X-Cube for 3D logic, H-Cube for 2.5D HBM) covers the major advanced packaging segments. The 4 μm hybrid bonding target for 2026 would accelerate the pitch-scaling race. However, Samsung's foundry packaging market share remains significantly below TSMC's, and CoWoS customer lock-in is a competitive barrier.
