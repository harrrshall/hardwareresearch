## Summary

TSMC's 3nm (N3 family) and 2nm (N2) nodes are the primary manufacturing platforms for leading-edge AI accelerators in 2025–2026. AI demand has driven TSMC to near-capacity utilization on N3, with N2 mass production commencing December 2025.

## N3 (3nm) Status

- **Monthly Capacity (April 2026):** 180,000 wafers — up 40%+ year-over-year
- **Primary Customers:** NVIDIA (Blackwell), AMD (MI350 series), Apple, Qualcomm
- **Capacity Status:** Tight; available capacity insufficient to meet demand
- **Revenue:** Among TSMC's highest-margin nodes

## N2 (2nm) Mass Production

- **Production Start:** December 31, 2025 (volume production)
- **Yield Rates:** Logic test chip yields stabilized at 70–80% — strong for new node
- **2026 Monthly Capacity Target:** ~100,000 wafers by end of 2026
- **Initial Customer Allocation:** Apple >50% of initial capacity; also Qualcomm, MediaTek, AMD, NVIDIA
- **Revenue Projection:** N2 set to surpass N3 and N5 in cumulative revenue by Q3 2026

## CoWoS Advanced Packaging

CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate) is the dominant packaging for AI accelerators:
- Target: 130,000 CoWoS wafers/month by late 2026 (quadruple current)
- Enables HBM stack attachment alongside GPU/TPU dies
- All Blackwell B200, H200, AMD MI350 series use CoWoS-L or CoWoS-S

## UCIe 3.0 Standard (August 2025)

- Ratified August 2025: interoperability for chiplets from different manufacturers
- UCIe-3D: vertical chiplet integration using hybrid bonding
- Orders-of-magnitude bandwidth increase vs planar 2.5D approaches
- Enables heterogeneous AI chips combining logic + analog + photonic dies

## Manufacturing Constraints Affecting AI Hardware

### HBM Constraints
- HBM3E prices up 171.8% YoY (late 2025)
- Shortage projected to last 2027–2030
- 20% price hike for 2026 supply (Samsung + SK Hynix)
- Goldman Sachs: most severe memory shortage in 15 years

### Wafer Capacity Competition
- N3 demand tightens as multiple AI chip generations compete for capacity
- Automotive and consumer electronics compete with AI for N3/N4
- Arizona Fab 2 (TSMC US): N3 volume production targeted 2H 2027

## Significance

TSMC's manufacturing roadmap directly gates AI accelerator advancement. The N2 ramp with strong yields positions 2026 as a pivotal year: Apple's M5/A20 and NVIDIA's Rubin will be the first consumer-scale 2nm products. The CoWoS capacity constraint explains why AI chip vendors are years ahead booking packaging capacity — it is as critical a bottleneck as wafer fabrication.
